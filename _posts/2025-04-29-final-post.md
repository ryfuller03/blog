It's the last one! Super bittersweet. To be honest, making these blog posts was never my favorite, but it's cool to look back on how much I have done and see what I was thinking while going through some tumultuous moments during projects. Would I have a blog while working on a solo project in the future? I don't know, maybe. We will see when the time comes!

This week, I FINALLY got the filtering to work on the Request Form page. There was a lot of disconnected logic for some reason, but I figured out it was due to inconsistency in my naming of IDs and names for HTML elements and linking that to the backend in C#. Finally, though, it is done, and I imagine it will be my last major contribution not just to this project but, unfortunately, to Disco Tray as a whole.

Disco Tray has been one of the best experiences I have ever received regarding actual expereince in the workplace and making a difference in peoples' lives. This kind of thing is exactly what I want to do for the rest of my life. It has been a great honor to work with everyone on the team and to make these projects come to life for those who need them. Thank you, Dr. Goadrich, for this opportunity--it made my college experience so much more worth it.
